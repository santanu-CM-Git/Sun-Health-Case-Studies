# Sun Health Case Study

## About project

Sun Health is a responsive WordPress website built for a long-standing Arizona nonprofit organization dedicated to healthy living and superior healthcare. The project involved translating comprehensive multi-page Figma designs into a fully functional, accessible web experience that reflects the organization's 55+ year commitment to serving West Valley residents.

## Problem

The platform faced multiple critical technical hurdles that compromised the user experience and site functionality:

- **Outdated Digital Presence:** Sun Health needed a modern website that could effectively communicate their mission of championing superior health and wellbeing to both current and prospective community members.
- **Complex Content Architecture:** With multiple service areas including philanthropy programs, inspired living initiatives, and wellness offerings, the site required intuitive navigation and clear information hierarchy across diverse audience segments.
- **Community Engagement Needs:** The organization required a platform that could effectively showcase their long-standing history while inspiring community members to engage with programs focused on living longer, healthier, and more purposeful lives.

## Technology stack

- **Platform:** WordPress
- **Page Builder:** Elementor
- **Design Source:** Figma Prototypes
- **Languages:** JavaScript, Custom CSS3

## The client

Sun Health is a respected Arizona nonprofit organization with over 55 years of service to West Valley residents. As a community-focused healthcare organization, they needed a digital platform that authentically represents their mission to champion superior health and wellbeing through philanthropy, inspired living, and wellness programs while supporting their vision of helping people live longer, healthier, and more purposeful lives.

## The solution

- **Figma-to-WordPress Implementation:** Precise translation of all multi-page Figma designs into a cohesive WordPress experience that maintains design integrity across devices.
- **Mission-Driven Design Architecture:** Structured content layout to clearly communicate Sun Health's mission, vision, and three core pillars—philanthropy, inspired living, and wellness programs.
- **Elementor Page Building:** Leveraged Elementor's native capabilities to create flexible, maintainable page templates that can evolve with the organization's growing program offerings.
- **Custom JavaScript Enhancements:** Implemented targeted JavaScript solutions to enhance user interactions and ensure smooth functionality across all pages.
- **Responsive Framework:** Engineered mobile-first responsive behavior to ensure West Valley community members can access health resources and program information seamlessly from any device.

## Result

- **Authentic Brand Representation:** Successfully captured Sun Health's 55+ year legacy while presenting a modern, trustworthy digital presence that resonates with community members.
- **Improved User Experience:** Created intuitive pathways for visitors to explore philanthropy opportunities, inspired living resources, and wellness programs aligned with their personal health goals.
- **Scalable Content Platform:** Delivered a maintainable WordPress solution that allows Sun Health staff to easily update program information, success stories, and community impact data.
- **Enhanced Community Connection:** Established a digital hub that effectively communicates Sun Health's vision of helping people live longer, healthier, and more purposeful lives, strengthening engagement with West Valley residents.
